    About FlowPilot

    We Don't Just Track Tasks
    We Help Teams Flow.

    The Founder & Team FlowPilot was started by Karthik Raja with a simple idea: teams lose time not because they lack effort, but because their work is scattered across too many tools. We set out to build one calm, clear workspace where plans, tasks, and conversations live together. No clutter, no confusion, just a straightforward way to move work forward. Every part of FlowPilot is designed around the same belief: when everyone can see what matters and what is next, great work happens naturally.
